The court's own words

"It is very clear from the amount of evidence presented by the prosecution that no direct evidence, testimonial or otherwise, linked CDT 1CL SANOPAO to the physical assault that ultimately led to the eventual death of the late CDT 4CL DORMITORIO."

GCM No. 2 Joint Decision, 31 March 2025, p. 75

On 31 March 2025, a general court-martial found Cadet Sanopao guilty of instigating the maltreatment that led to the death of a fourth-class cadet, Darwin D. Dormitorio. In the same decision, the court admitted more than once that no direct evidence and no eyewitness ever placed such an order in his mouth.

Every fact on these pages is drawn from the court's own decision, the sworn transcripts, the charge sheet, or the civilian prosecutor's resolutions. Nothing here is added; the citations are shown so any reader can check them.
