The medicine

After he wasgone.

The hospital observed Dormitorio for hours that day and found no fresh injury. The defense pathologist testified that the fatal injuries could not have been present then, and the court's own NBI witness, asked by a panel member, placed the massive injury after the discharge.

17 September, in the hospital

Seven hours, no fresh injury

The forensic experts

When the fatal injury came

Earlier in the same testimony, under the Law Member's questions, Dr. Bandonill allowed that some injuries could date from the morning of 17 September (pp. 8–9). Asked by the panel to account for the massive injuries, he placed them after the discharge (p. 17).
